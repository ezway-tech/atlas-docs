# Little Café image assets

[Documentation home](../README.md)

Paths below are relative to the repository root.

Created using the built-in OpenAI ImageGen tool. These are generated photographic-style images, not photographs of a real café. Both outputs are 1536 × 1024 PNGs. Existing asset IDs and filenames are preserved for compatibility.

## Menu cover

Saved to `examples/little-cafe/uploads/cafe0000-0000-4000-8000-000000000001.png`.

Prompt:

Create a photorealistic editorial photograph, landscape 3:2, for the fictional Little Café menu cover. Real artisan cafe oak counter in warm natural window light. A beautiful small dark green menu board upright at center with elegant perfectly readable cream lettering, exact text: LITTLE CAFÉ / Espresso $2.50 / Latte $4.00 / Iced tea $3.00. A real ivory ceramic latte with delicate rosetta foam in foreground, a small espresso and tall iced amber tea nearby. Subtle blurred espresso machine and warm cafe interior behind. Tactile wood, natural ceramic imperfections, convincing drink textures, professional food photography, tasteful restrained styling. Keep menu board and drinks grouped in the central 65 percent so a square center crop works. No illustrations, flat vector shapes, fake UI, collage, watermark or extra words. Save generated image to disk for use in the project.
