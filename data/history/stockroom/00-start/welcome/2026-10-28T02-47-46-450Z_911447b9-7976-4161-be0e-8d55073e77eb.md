# Welcome

Stockroom is a small web app for parts on a shelf.

A clerk receives a box, puts it on a shelf, and issues it when a job needs it.

## A normal day

1. A delivery arrives at the dock.
2. The clerk scans each part and chooses a shelf.
3. The app adds the quantity on that shelf.
4. When a job starts, the clerk issues the parts and the count goes down.
