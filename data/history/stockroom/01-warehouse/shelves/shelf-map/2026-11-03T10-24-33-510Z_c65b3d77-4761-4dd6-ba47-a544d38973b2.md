# Shelf map

The North Shed has two aisles. Aisle A faces the dock. Aisle B faces the jobs bench.

![Shelf map of aisles A and B](/api/media/4f8c1a20-6b14-4c3e-9a11-0c0ffee00003)

| Code | Aisle | Holds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A1 | A | Fasteners |
| A2 | A | Boxes of fittings |
| B1 | B | Hand tools |
| B2 | B | Gloves and safety gear |

A movement names a shelf code. See [stock movements](../movements.md) and the [items API](../../02-platform/openapi.yml).
