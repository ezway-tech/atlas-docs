## Coffee
Use the house espresso blend. Label the open bag with its opening date and keep it sealed between shifts.

## Milk choices
Use dairy milk or oat milk. Keep separate, labeled pitchers and ask the customer which they prefer. Follow the supplier storage instructions.

## Tea
Brew the house tea according to the pack instructions and label the batch.

## Related work
Use these ingredients in the [latte recipe](latte.md). The shift lead records quantities in the [inventory guide](../operations/stock/inventory.md).
