## Ingredients
- 18 g ground coffee
- 36 g espresso yield
- 180 ml milk

Use dairy milk or oat milk.

![Latte with rosetta foam](/api/media/cafe0000-0000-4000-8000-000000000002)

## Method
1. Weigh and grind the coffee.
2. Pull a double espresso into a warm cup.
3. Steam the milk and pour it over the espresso.
4. Call the order number and hand over the drink.

## Quality check
The drink should taste **balanced**, with *smooth foam*. Record a recipe adjustment in a comment before changing the shared instructions.

> Confirm the milk choice before steaming. A clear handover matters as much as a good recipe.

### Taste and texture
Check one thing at a time:
- Espresso
  - Look for a steady extraction.
  - Taste before adjusting the grind.
- Milk
  - Keep the foam smooth.
  - Pour immediately after steaming.

#### Adjust the next cup
If the drink tastes too strong, check the measured ingredients before changing the recipe.

##### Record the adjustment
Leave a short comment describing the change and its result.

Tell the next barista which adjustment worked. This deepest heading also lets you try the H6 table-of-contents filter.
