Little Café is a fictional neighborhood café with six tables, three drinks, and a two-person team. All prices are in USD. All names, orders, and media are demo content.

![Little Café menu board](/api/media/cafe0000-0000-4000-8000-000000000001)

## Your first visit
Start with [Explore Atlas Docs](explore.md), then open the [menu](../menu/menu.md). Follow a drink from its [recipe](../menu/latte.md) through the [order flow](../operations/order-flow.md).

## Who uses this library?
- The barista checks recipes and the opening checklist.
- The shift lead checks ingredients and stock.
- The developer maintains the small ordering app.

## A safe place to experiment
Edit this demo freely. It is a copy of the bundled example, not the example itself. Export a backup before experimenting with restore or deletion.
